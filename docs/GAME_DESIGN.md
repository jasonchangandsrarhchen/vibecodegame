# PROJECT FRONTIER — Game Design Document
Version 0.2.0 · Playable automation prototype

## High concept
A real-time, top-down planetary colony builder. The player commands a stranded expedition and turns a hostile, resource-rich world into a self-sustaining industrial frontier.

## Design pillars
1. **Visible growth:** a small landing zone becomes a sprawling, readable industrial complex.
2. **Interlocking systems:** extraction, power, processing, assembly, research, storage and defense depend on one another.
3. **Meaningful constraints:** limited land, finite starting stockpiles, day/night generation and escalating threats create trade-offs.
4. **Player-directed automation:** the player chooses what to build and where; production continues while the simulation runs.
5. **Expandable depth:** each milestone adds a connected system, not an isolated menu.

## Camera and controls
- 2D top-down grid, real-time simulation.
- Mouse click selects a tile; construction selection followed by tile click places a building.
- Pause and simulation speed controls.
- Browser localStorage save.

## Resource chain
- Iron ore → smelter → alloy plates.
- Copper ore + alloy plates → assembler → electronic components.
- Crystal deposits → crystal drill → research and advanced construction.
- Power is generated by solar arrays and wind turbines; batteries increase storage.
- Research labs convert operating time and power into research points.

## Buildings
| Building | Function |
|---|---|
| Miner | Extracts iron or copper from its tile |
| Solar array | High daytime power generation |
| Wind turbine | Continuous lower output |
| Smelter | Converts iron ore into alloy plates |
| Assembler | Converts plates and copper into electronics |
| Crystal drill | Extracts crystal |
| Conveyor | Planned logistics routing marker |
| Battery | Expands energy storage |
| Turret | Automatically resolves alien raids |
| Research lab | Generates research points |

## Progression
The opening mission establishes mining and power. The second milestone introduces alloy production. The third asks for electronics and a research lab. The open-ended phase emphasizes research, expansion and defense.

## Threat model
Alien raids arrive on a repeating planetary cycle and grow stronger over time. Turrets reduce incoming raid strength. Remaining damage reduces colony integrity. Later milestones will add enemy archetypes, target selection, repair systems and active defense.

## Research
- Efficient Mining: increased extraction.
- Energy Engineering: increased generation.
- Advanced Metallurgy: faster smelting.
- Pulse Weapons: stronger turret defense.
- Industrial Automation: faster assembly.
- Planetary Shield: colony protection and recovery.

## Visual direction
Dark planetary terrain, muted green plains, cyan UI, warm mineral deposits, bright machine silhouettes, and readable warning colors. Favor clarity and legible production states over decorative complexity.

## Planned systems
- Actual conveyor item transport and directional junctions.
- Fluid extraction, pipes, pumps and refining.
- Storage buildings and configurable production priorities.
- Larger maps, fog of war, exploration and resource outposts.
- More alien archetypes, raids with visible units, repair and advanced defenses.
- Sound, settings, accessibility, save export/import and balance testing.
